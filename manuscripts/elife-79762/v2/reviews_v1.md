## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.79762.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.79762.sa0)

The authors investigate the upstream regulators of axolotl Fgf8 expression in limb development and regeneration with the aim of identifying the reasons for its unique mesenchymal localization. The results suggest some refractoriness of the axolotl epidermis to activate Wnt signaling although Fgf8 expression in the anterior mesenchyme is still under Wnt control.
