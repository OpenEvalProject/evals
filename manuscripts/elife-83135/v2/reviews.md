DOI: [10.7554/eLife.83135.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.83135.sa0)

Trypanosomes are unusual in the way that they transcribe protein-coding genes. This work defines the role of the SPARC complex in transcription and highlights the role of potential histone readers and writers. This work will be of interest to those in the kinetoplastid community, especially those working on the control of gene expression.
