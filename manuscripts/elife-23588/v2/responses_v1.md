Reviewer #3:

Overall this is a well-written and well organized manuscript that identifies Pou3f2 as an important transcriptional regulator of the endothelial lineage. Strategies and data obtained (as outlined in the manuscript) will be a benefit to the field. The heterokaryon strategy is interesting and as shown by the authors it was effective in identifying endothelial transcriptional activators. Although this is an excellent manuscript, additional experiments should be included to further enhance the impact of the discovery.
