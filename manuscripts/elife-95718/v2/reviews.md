## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.95718.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.95718.sa0)

This important work features an innovative proximity labeling approach to the identification of proteins enriched in distinct types of chromatin domains. The work further shows that selective protein interactions are RNA dependent. This compelling evidence may fundamentally advance our understanding of chromosome domains and the role of RNA in organizing them.
