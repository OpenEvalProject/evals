The manuscript was considerably improved compared with the previous version addressing the reviewer's comments. However, I feel that the conclusions authors are trying to make solely from the computational results are too strong.

The authors compare two alternative models that ensures Fzd7 expression that causes thin Wnt activation domain: Wnt/Fzd7 feedback and Wnt-independent Fzd7 expression. The authors argue that the results of computer simulations prefer the former model. However, I have an impression that both models are possible.
