## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.82343.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.82343.sa0)

This important study demonstrates a conserved function of somatic human DCL3/ Drosophila Cv-c in the development of the testis. The evidence supporting the claims of the authors is compelling as they combine the human genetics of a patient with gonadal dysgenesis with genetic rescues in Drosophila. This work will be of interest to developmental biologists and human geneticists. A major strength of the paper is proving that the presumptive lipid-interacting StART domain (but not the GAP domain) of DCL3/Cv-c is required for testis development, which opens a new area of investigation.
