6) As the authors cannot identify postsynaptic densities, can they exclude SFL input from not traced neurons to SAMs?

Whenever we traced a SAM, we carefully annotated all its synapses along the way. Annotating synapses does not require tracing the pre- or postsynaptic cell elements of the SAM being reconstructed. Hence, accordingly, we are confident that all SFL inputs of the fully reconstructed SAMs were accurately annotated (within the volume; some of the SAM’s neuritic ending may have been cut short). Synaptic inputs from the AF to the SAM are more discrete, and we cannot exclude a slight underestimation of these synapses.
