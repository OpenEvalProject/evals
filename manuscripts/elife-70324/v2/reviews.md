Acceptance summary:

This paper, which documents clearly the dual role of hypothalamic NYP in the regulation of bone and fat metabolism, is conceptually novel and is a valuable extension to the evolving concept of how the skeleton is connected with the brain. Potential therapeutic implications may emerge from this study.
