DOI: [10.7554/eLife.102658.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.102658.sa0)

The present study presents important findings, establishing that the yeast system of ER to cytosol reflux of ER-resident proteins upon ER stress is conserved in mammalian cells. Convincing evidence further link between ER stress, protein reflux and inhibition of p53, and thus affect cancer cell survival in the face of ER-stress, with implications relevant to cancer biology.
