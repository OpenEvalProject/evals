Acceptance summary:

This work is significant because it challenges the widely held belief that essential genes are highly conserved over evolutionary time by demonstrating that a rapidly evolving family of transcription factors has essential functions in Drosophila. It also beautifully demonstrates how an essential gene in D. melanogaster that interacts with heterochromatin has functionally diverged from its ortholog in D. simulans. Overall, the study is impressive for its breadth and depth, and because it provides important new insights into how new gene functions evolve.
