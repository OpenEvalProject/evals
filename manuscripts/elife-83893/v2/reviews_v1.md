## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.83893.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.83893.sa0)

This important work builds on the conceptual framework of host-pathogen interactions and co-evolution, adding new examples of co-divergence of primate herpesviruses with their respective host restriction factors. The authors convincingly outline the degree to which their initial findings (BORF2 and A3B interactions) are conserved across other herpesvirus RNRs, and place them in the context of the evolution of the A3 gene locus and expansion. This work will be of great interest to virologists. Especially those that work in the field of host pathogen evolution and the molecular arms race.
