## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.74094.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.74094.sa0)

Utilizing the zebrafish models and the state-of-art approaches, this study clearly identifies that the arterial mural cells serve as the source for the chemokines and growth factors, which are key factors not only for the migration and survival of lymphatic endothelial cells but also for the building of lymphatic networks in most organs during embryonic development. The findings and conclusion would be an important platform in the future design for rebuilding lymphatic vessels in treating lymphatic-deficient diseases including lymphedema.
