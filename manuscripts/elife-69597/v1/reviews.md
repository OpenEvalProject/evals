Acceptance summary:

Live-cell microscopy of Drosophila larvae shows that actin patches assemble transiently in unstimulated neuromuscular junctions at the frequency of spontaneous synaptic vesicle release. Disruption of the interactions between Dap160 and Nwk in vivo leads to dysregulated and more frequent actin assembly. The authors propose that these interactions serve as a 'clamp' on the actin assembly machinery, so that it can be spatially and temporally controlled when needed for synaptic vesicle endocytosis.
