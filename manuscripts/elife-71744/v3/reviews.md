Acceptance summary:

This paper tackles the question of whether the distribution of signaling ligands in cells of the wing disc is regulated analogous to neurotransmitters at chemical neuronal synapses. Specifically is the distribution of Hh, Wg, and Dpp dependent and proportional to the amount produced? The data suggest that delivery of these ligands to target cells is regulated in both amount and destination.
