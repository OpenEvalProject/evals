Panel A: It seems that ZCWPW1 correlates poorly with cPRDM9. Why is this?

Overall, ZCWPW1 in cells co-transfected with cPRDM9 still exhibits a large and clear enrichment at cPRDM9 sites, so we certainly do see allele-specific co-localisation. However, there are quantitative differences, with ZCWPW1 showing weaker enrichment for cPRDM9 than hPRDM9, in co-transfected cells.
