Acceptance summary:

This is a very nice study, which exploits the trypanosome system to investigate the role of mRNA-specific mRNPs in regulating protein (VSG) expression. The experimental approaches used are robust, the data clear and complete. The findings are of broad interest to anyone interested in understanding post-transcriptional mechanisms, as well as being of high interest to parasitologists
