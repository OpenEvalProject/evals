## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.80690.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.80690.sa0)

This study describing how continuous perioperative IFNα therapy stimulates hepatic endothelial cells to build up a physical vascular barrier that limits tumor cell entry into the liver and promotes long-term antitumor immunity provides novel evidence for anti-metastatic effects of IFNα via effects on the liver vascular compartment. This work is predicted to advance the field and will be of interest to scientists studying cancer, inflammation and liver function.
