“Indeed, ORNs housed in the same sensillum can inhibit each other by means of direct electrical interaction, termed ephaptic coupling, which can also modulate fruitfly behavior in response to odor mixtures (Su, Menuz, Reisert, and Carlson, 2012; Zhang et al., 2019).
