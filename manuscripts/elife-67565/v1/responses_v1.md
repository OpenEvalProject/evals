1. As we state in the methods, our assay was based on an established protocol for plants (Li et al., 2019; Roeske and Oleary 1985; Sharkey et al., 1986), with the slight adjustments to make the protocol suitable specific for Chlamydomonas. In plants, the normalization is usually done according to leaf area (Li et al., 2019). b. Light intensities were set to be equal between the samples;

c. The turbidostat function of the photobioreactor held the cells at approximately the same densities. For transparency, we show biomass measurements (ash free dry weight) for CC1009 and CC2343 across our hyperoxia treatment. Note that the turbidostat will maintain approximately the same cell density even when growth rates differ.
