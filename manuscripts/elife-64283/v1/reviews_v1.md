Acceptance summary:

The high-resolution biochemical experiments reported here demonstrate that defects in recycling ribosomes that have finished synthesizing a protein result in the efficient translation of a subset of messenger RNAs that have been previously shown to be efficiently translated under conditions in which initiation of translation is impaired. The results have implications for our understanding of the translational control of gene expression and, possibly, our understanding of human diseases caused by defects in the concentration of ribosomes available for initiation.
