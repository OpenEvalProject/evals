Summary:

This concise contribution reports a new EM structure that allows a more accurate description and understanding of the Ctf19 complex. Specifically, the authors studied a fragment of Ctf3, Mcm16, and Mcm22 roughly encompassing half of the Ctf3 complex (the N-terminal halves of Mcm16 and Mcm22, and the C-terminal half of Ctf3). At the resolution of the map, the authors were finally able to complete a univocal assignment of sequence to density. The resulting model supersedes, in various ways, the original assignments for this sub-complex initially released with a recent eLife paper by the same authors (including reversing the direction of the Mcm16 and Mcm22 helices).
