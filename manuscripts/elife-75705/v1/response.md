i) As pointed out in line 364, there have now been a few reports about the pandemic's effect on the workforce, including postdocs. Our findings highlight the importance of institutional resources for the mental health and well-being of the postdoctoral population. This is our main takeaway message – “As we22 and others have previously reported, postdocs are an often overlooked and forgotten population in academia, with a non-negligible number of institutions being unaware of their total postdoc population, let alone the concerns of postdocs. Access to employee benefits varies widely1-3, with untoward consequences for postdocs who lack access to certain essential services.
