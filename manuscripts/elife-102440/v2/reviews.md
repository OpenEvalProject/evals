## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.102440.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.102440.sa0)

This work represents a significant milestone in understanding the regulatory logic underlying arterial identity. It offers an exceptional repository of validated arterial enhancers that drive expression across different vascular compartments. Moreover, it identifies the upstream transcription factor binding sites within these arterial enhancers. As such, this study will be of broad interest to developmental biologists, vascular researchers, and cell biologists.
