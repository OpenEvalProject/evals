## Abstract

An AuDHD researcher recounts the highs and lows of relocating from the United States to Germany for his postdoc.

## About this article

This Sparks of Change article is part of a series of articles on being neurodivergent in academia, which includes a list of tips, resources and tools collated by neurodivergent scientists.
