## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.79128.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.79128.sa0)

This study provides evidence for a novel function of S6K1 in DNA repair. The findings explain how amplification of RPS6KB1 gene, which encodes the S6K1 protein, in breast cancers is associated with a poor outcome after chemotherapy. The conclusions of the paper are well-supported by the experimental results. The proteomics data leading to the discovery of Cdk1 and Msh6 as targets of S6K1 are novel and important for the cancer research field.
