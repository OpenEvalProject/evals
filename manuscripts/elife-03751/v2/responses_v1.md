We completely agree with the reviewers’ interpretation of the data and have rephrased our conclusions to express this more clearly. The non-catalytic function of AKT requires the presence of the PH domain, as demonstrated by our data with the Δ PH-Myr-Akt1-K179M mutant. Together with the results of our experiments using the R86C and R25C mutants, this data suggest that the kinase-independent function of AKT might require targeting to specific membranes or membrane subdomains, likely those richest in PI(4,5)P2, to which the mutants show enhanced binding.
