## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.92409.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.92409.sa0)

This article reports on hitherto unrecognized adaptors of p97/VCP, which is a multifunctional ATPase that unwinds diverse protein substrates subserving important roles in cell physiology. The adaptors in question, members of the FAM104 family, direct p97 to the nucleus to enable unwinding events in that location. The findings, which are supported by solid experimental observations, are valuable and will inform the work of the sizable community that studies various aspects of p97/VCP.
