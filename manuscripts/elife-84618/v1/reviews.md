## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.84618.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.84618.sa0)

This paper investigates the role of lysine-specific histone demethylase 1 (LSD1) in skeletal muscle responses to stresses. The findings link LSD1 to responses of skeletal muscle to glucorticoids and exercise in a fiber-type-dependent manner. The paper should be of broad interest to those studying muscle biology and physiology.
