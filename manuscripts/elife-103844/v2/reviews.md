## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.103844.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.103844.sa0)

This work describes the effect of Optineurin (OPTN) mutations in the transcellular degradation of retinal ganglion cell mitochondria by astrocytes in the Optic Nerve, a process termed, "transcellular degradation of mitochondria". The authors perform compelling live imaging studies of the Xenopus laevis optic nerve to track neuronal mitochondrial movement and expulsion in an intact nervous system. These important findings demonstrate that Optineurin mutations that are associated with disease increase the stationary pool of mitochondria resulting in increased rates of transcellular degradation.
