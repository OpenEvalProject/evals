Acceptance summary:

NAFLD while of profound physiological significance to human health remains somewhat mysterious in terms of its cause, which limits new therapeutic approaches. This paper links hepatocyte proliferation to dysregulation of lipid metabolism and proposes a new mechanism to explain the development of age-dependent NAFLD. It is an important paper for the field and should be of widespread interest- particularly researchers in the fields of liver disease, ageing and lipid metabolism.
