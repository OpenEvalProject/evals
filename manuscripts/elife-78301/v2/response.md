Thanks for the insightful comments. We have conducted the suggested experiment. As shown in Figure 4—figure supplement 2, the inside-out recordings from TRPV2-expressing HEK293T cells at +60 mV show that the presence of Mg2+ increased the 2-APB response. However, the excised membrane patches might attach portion of tyrosine kinase JAK1. This confirms the Mg2+-induced TRPV2 current enhancement is modulated by JAK1 phosphorylation.

In addition, we tested the effect of Mg2+ on inside-out recordings of

TRPV2(Y335/471/525F) mutant that loses the capability to be phosphorylated by JAK1. As expected, Mg2+ failed to enhance the 2-APB-evoked currents. Together, these data corroborate the Mg2+-JAK1-mediated phosphorylation contributes to the increased sensitivity of the TRPV2 channel. Of note, our findings also suggest that though exerting a facilitatory effect, Mg2+ alone is unable to activate TRPV2, and the excised membrane patches cannot completely isolate the regulatory effect of the intracellular signaling pathway occurring on underneath the cell membrane site.

Although the anti-Flag agarose was repeatedly washed to remove the proteins that interacted with JAK1, some of the interaction proteins remained and were phosphorylated during in vitro kinase assay, resulting in dispersion bands of varying sizes.
