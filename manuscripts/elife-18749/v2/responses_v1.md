Reviewer #1:

This paper by Tiriac and Blumberg bears on the gating of re-afferent signals during forelimb movements generated during waking versus forelimb twitches generated during deep sleep in rat pups. It is reported that gating of re-afferent signals occurs during waking, but not during twitches, and that gating occurs in the dorsal column nuclei (the external cuneate nucleus).

[…]

Reviewer #2:

Authors explored a critical component of their developing story implicating limb twitching during sleep as having functional significance.

[…]

We have also done the following:

4) Altered the title of the paper to "Gating of reafference in the external cuneate nucleus during self-generated movements in wake but not sleep"; and

5) Made several small stylistic edits to the text.
