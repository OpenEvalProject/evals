## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.90499.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.90499.sa0)

This interesting study provides valuable data exploring how progenitors control their contribution to somitogenesis. By combining classic embryology techniques with single-cell sequencing, the authors describe novel cell states that might help understand progenitor population dynamics. This manuscript will be of interest to researchers in the development field who want to better understand hox control and influence during axial elongation.
