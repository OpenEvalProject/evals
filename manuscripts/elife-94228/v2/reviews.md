## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.94228.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.94228.sa0)

The molecular mechanisms of mammalian gamete fusion are still obscure. In this study, the authors provide compelling evidence that binding of sperm to somatic cells expressing egg counterpart proteins enables non-reproductive cells to efficiently undergo cell-cell fusion and form a syncytium. This is an important finding that establishes a new experimental system to investigate the proteins involved in egg-sperm binding, and could accelerate innovation of male infertility diagnostic assays.
