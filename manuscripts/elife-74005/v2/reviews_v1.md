Acceptance summary:

This study significantly advances our understanding of how eusocial organisms communicate with each other within a colony by studying proteins in trophallactic fluids extracted from carpenter ants. These proteins help coordinate physiology and development among individuals in the hive. The methods used here are cutting edge and the work is expected to provide a foundation for many future studies.
