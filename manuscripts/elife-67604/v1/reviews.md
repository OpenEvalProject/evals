Acceptance summary:

The autophagic turnover of mitochondria is termed mitophagy. This study provides the first in vivo evidence that pathogenic, Parkinson's disease-associated LRRK2 kinase directly impairs basal mitophagy, and demonstrates that pharmacological inhibition of LRRK2 is a rational mitophagy-rescue approach and potential Parkinson's disease therapy.
