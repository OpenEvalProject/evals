Acceptance summary:

This paper makes use of an elegant and technically complex approach to study the T cell receptor (TCR) clonotype dynamics of follicular helper T (Tfh) cell during an auto-antigen challenge, which is done by laser capturing of germinal centres inside popliteal lymph nodes, and combined with TCR sequencing, clearly isolating differentiated Tfh cells.
