## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.82669.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.82669.sa0)

This research innovatively depicted that CD81 could form interaction with CD44 and contributes to the self-renewal, and metastatic capacity of breast cancer stem cells. Through large amounts of validation, either through computational or experimental methods, the conclusions are firmly held back that CD81-CD44 interaction is indispensable for circulating tumor cell dissemination and metastasis. The findings have broadened the knowledge of the molecular regulatory network of breast cancer cell stemness and may be significant for scholars who are experts in cancer biology.
