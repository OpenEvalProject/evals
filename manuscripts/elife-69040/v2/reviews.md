Acceptance summary:

This study describes a detailed analysis of gene expression in Plasmodium falciparum, malaria causing parasite, isolated from a group of adult travelers returning to Germany. The authors developed new approaches that allowed them to confirm the presence of particular parasite variants, whereby one is associated with severe malaria and the other with non-severe malaria infection. This is a comprehensive study with an interesting dataset and the findings highlight the key knowledge gap about variant antigenic expression in adult severe malaria.
