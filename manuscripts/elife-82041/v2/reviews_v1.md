## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.82041.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.82041.sa0)

This paper describes a mechanistic and structural investigation of a crucial step in exocytosis that involves the assembly of two t-SNAREs with a v-SNARE. The authors solve the structure of Sec3, a component of the exocyst vesicle tethering complex and the t-SNARE Sso2, and identify a previously unknown binding site that helps to explain the mechanism of this rate-limiting step. The results are of great interest to scientists studying exocytosis, membrane fusion, and protein-protein interaction.
