DOI: [10.7554/eLife.80273.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.80273.sa0)

This work provides a major contribution to the field of oligodendrogenesis. It is a comprehensive analysis of Tensin3 function and is conceptually novel as it links the fields of transcriptional control in oligodendrocyte lineage cells with morphogenetic changes and integrin signaling-mediated cell survival. Finally, it is also the discovery of a very useful marker for remyelinating oligodendrocytes in disease conditions.
