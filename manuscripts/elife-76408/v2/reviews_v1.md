## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.76408.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.76408.sa0)

The authors probe the role of multipolar migration of horizontal cells in the zebrafish retina. The results reveal amoeboid-like migration enabling cell movements to adapt to environmental spatial constraints in the crowded retina including cell and nuclear shape changes and rearward polarization of stable F-actin.
