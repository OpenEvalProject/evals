## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.88329.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.88329.sa0)

This is an important study investigating interactions of the pro-apoptotic PUMA with anti-apoptotic BCL-2 proteins. The authors demonstrate convincingly that the PUMA/BCL-2 interactions are mediated not only via BH3-domain interaction, but also depend on a C-terminal sequence of PUMA similar to BIM. Unexpectedly they find PUMA is often localising to the ER. This manuscript is important for researchers focusing on cell death and anti-tumor drug development.
