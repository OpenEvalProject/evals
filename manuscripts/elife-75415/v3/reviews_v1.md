## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.75415.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.75415.sa0)

This is a well designed and well conducted study comparing ~450 human DNA repair protein and protein fragments in fusions with CRISPR/Cas9 for their efficiency in HDR genome editing and found that Cas9-POLD3 performed best in screening system or local targeting. The work proved that Cas9-POLD3 enhances editing at the early time points by speeding up the kinetics of Cas9 DNA binding and dissociation, allowing the rapid initiation of DNA repair. This work will be of interest to those wanting to improve gene editing efficiency by HDR.
