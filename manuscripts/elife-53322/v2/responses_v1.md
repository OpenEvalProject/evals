## Response text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.53322.sa2](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.53322.sa2)

Essential revisions:

1) A biochemistry figure or figure supplement is lacking (e.g. showing the quality of the BBSome preparation). This is especially missed in Results paragraph one, where biochemical results are discussed.

We have added a panel (Figure 1—figure supplement 1a) showing the purity of the BBSome after purification.

2) In the submitted PDB files, the coordinates for the Ca2+ ion near BBS2 propeller blade 6 appear to be severely misplaced (cf. Figure 2e).

We apologize for this misplacement, which must have happened as we were preparing the files for submission. We have corrected the positioning in the final models deposited to the PDB.

3) The conformational change coupled with ARL6 binding is arguably the key result of the paper, but I think could be more clearly illustrated in Figure 6. Perhaps a panel plotting RMSD on the structure between states or showing inter-atomic displacement vectors could help depict the motion. The authors may also have their own ideas.

We have added a new figure (Figure 6—figure supplement 1) showing the position of BBS1 in the unbound and ARL6-bound states, as well as two views of the conformational change displayed as inter-atomic displacement vectors.
