Acceptance summary:

This is a very interesting paper that uncovers lipid droplet (LD) content of tumor cells derived from different types of cancer as impacting radio-sensitivity. They suggest that tumor cell heterogeneity in LD is pre-existing and selected for and that iron imbalance and FTH1 are key mediators. They show that silencing of FTH1 enhances radiosensitivity. This paper has broad interest in the field of cancer.
