6) L215: There is a double band in the anti Flag Western Blot. Do the authors have any idea why there are two bands?

In figure 4c, we notice only band with heme staining (top panel) and two bands in the anti-flag western blot (bottom panel). It is likely that the second band is a degradation product of the tagged version of CcmE that does not bind heme. We do not notice any cross-reactivity with the anti-FLAG antibody in the strain expressing the C120H mutant of CcmE (which likely does not produce a stable protein), which further corroborates our hypothesis that the second band in the anti-FLAG western is a CcmE degradation product rather than any other protein in the cell.
