Thank you for choosing to send your work entitled “Sequence Specific Detection of Bacterial 23S Ribosomal RNA by TLR13” for consideration at eLife. Your article has been fully evaluated by a Senior Editor and 1 other reviewer. The following individuals responsible for the peer review of your submission want to reveal their identity: Tada Taniguchi (senior editor and the reviewing editor for this submission); Osamu Takeuchi (peer reviewer).

The Reviewing Editor and the other reviewer discussed their comments before we reached this decision, and the Reviewing Editor has assembled the following comments based on the reviewers' reports. Our goal is to provide the essential revision requirements as a single set of instructions, so that you have a clear view of the revisions that are necessary for us to publish your work.

This is a very interesting paper that describes the sequence-specific recognition of bacterial 23S ribosomal RNA by a Toll-like receptor 11 (TLR11) family, TLR13. Although the basic principle for recognition of "non-self" by innate receptors has been for pattern recognition, as opposed to molecular recognition, of microbe-derived molecules, TLR13 is apparently different. TLR13 instead recognizes specific, conserved sequences of the 23S RNA that are essential for the catalytic center of peptide bond formation in gram-negative and -positive bacteria. Therefore, this provides a new view of innate receptor recognition.

We will be happy to accept this manuscript, provided that the authors make a single, quick round of revision by taking the following issues raised by the referees.

(1) The authors should mention, succinctly, the similarity and novelty of their current findings in respect to the paper that recently appeared in Science Express (19 July 2012 / http://www.sciencemag.org/content/337/6098/1111.abstract).

(2) Throughout the study, the only output examined for the TLR13-MyD88 signaling by 23S rRNA or ISR23 is the induction of IL-1β mRNA. It is most likely that this signal induces other genes such as those for TNF-α, IL6 and type I IFNs. It would be preferable that they show mRNA induction for at least one or two of these cytokines, so that the readers will not misinterpret that the TLR13-MyD88 pathway is specifically targeted to IL-1β gene in lieu of a broader response, assuming this is the case.

(3) Is there any possibility that TLR13 signals in conjunction with other TLRs? It would be interesting to discuss this issue.
