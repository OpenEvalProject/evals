We appreciate your careful review of our paper. 7. Line 198: Why the author did not investigate the other three nucleoside-related genes for further validation? Are there any plans for this?

Thank you for your suggestion. Our in vitro assay showed that nucleosides were hydrolyzed to purine bases, prompting us to focus on the iunH, rihA, and rihC genes. While previous studies have established the nucleoside-hydrolyzing functions of rihA and rihC [1, 2], the function of iunH remains unknown. Thus, we will concentrate on iunH and plan to further investigate the interactions among these three genes.
