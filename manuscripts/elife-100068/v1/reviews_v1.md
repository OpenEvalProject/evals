## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.100068.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.100068.sa0)

Multiple studies have shown that the gut microbiota is involved in the metabolism of uric acid and influences systemic uric acid levels. However, the specific bacteria and genes involved in this process are not known. The current study provides convincing evidence that Lactobacillus plantarum prevents the synthesis of uric acid through the hydrolysis of nucleosides through the nucleoside hydrolase gene (iunH). This marks a valuable contribution to the field.
