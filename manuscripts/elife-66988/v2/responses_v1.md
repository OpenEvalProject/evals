We found that while the work is both novel and very relevant to the field, there were several concerns regarding the interpretation of results and the subsequent claims that make this not suitable for publication. Some of the major issues involve the way in which data was collected and presented (such as MICs, evidence for OMVs, experiments with stool samples, and statistics). Most relevant, however, is the lack of convincing evidence for the role of OMVs in polymyxin resistance, such as polymyxin binding or changes in the LPS, to support the claim for OMV protection in this species.

Reviewer #1:
