Acceptance summary:

This manuscript is of interest to those studying synaptic molecular mechanisms underlying learning. It identifies a key role for a particular class of developmentally regulated glutamate receptor in the activity-dependent regulation of dendritic protein synthesis. Multiple genetic, biochemical, molecular biological experiments strongly support the existence of the molecular mechanism proposed by the authors. Behavioral experiments with mutant mice suggest, but do not yet prove, that the mechanism contributes to memory formation in adult animals.

Decision letter after peer review:
