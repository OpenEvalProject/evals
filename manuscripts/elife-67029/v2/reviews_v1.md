Acceptance summary:

This manuscript provides a murine model of Rickettsia parkeri infection, specifically for eschar formation upon rickettsiae infection. The researchers employed employed an intradermal (i.d.) infection model for infection using mice that were deficient in two interferon receptors (Ifnar-/-Ifngr-/-). Their study further describe new molecular mechanisms for rickettsiae infection during eschar formation, thereby, advancing our understanding on Rickettsia-Host Cell interaction.
