## Response text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.07236.017](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.07236.017)

1) Is there a reason why a Ramachandran Analysis was not included in the refinement statistics for both structures?

While refining each structure, we used Ramachandran analysis to ensure the chemical feasibility of our models. The analysis didn’t highlight any issues with our finalised structures, and all modelled residues fall within allowed regions, with all (Ana2 CCCD), or the vast majority (for SAS-6) in favoured regions of Ramachandran space. We initially did not include this information for brevity, but we have now expanded the crystallographic tables to include this information.

2) In Figure 5A, it would be helpful to mention what antibody was used to detect the Ana2 constructs. The same goes for Sas-6 in Figure 7A.

We have now more clearly listed all antibodies (along with their relevant manufacturers/citations) including rabbit anti-Ana2 (3:500), (Stevens et al., 2010a), and rabbit anti-Sas-6 (1:500) (Basto et al., 2006) in the Materials and methods. Additionally, we have now added “α” symbols into Figures 5A and 7A to clarify which antibodies were used on each blot.

We have also corrected a few minor mistakes, added some references that were missing and amended the text to take into account some work that was published while our paper was under review (most noticeably the Kratz et al., study looking at the interaction between STIL and Plk4).
