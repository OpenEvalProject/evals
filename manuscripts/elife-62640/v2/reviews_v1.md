Acceptance summary:

TRIM37 E3 ubiquitin ligase deficiency causes the rare disorder Mulibrey nanism. This work provides first insights into the formation and regulation of centriole-like assemblies that form in cells lacking TRIM37 activity. By functioning as ectopic MTOCs these assemblies can interfere with bipolar mitotic spindle assembly, causing chromosome segregation errors.
