In the interests of transparency, eLife publishes the most substantive revision requests and the accompanying author responses.

Acceptance summary:

Some voltage-gated ion channels are opened by hyperpolarizing (more negative) membrane potentials and others open with depolarization. This differential gating polarity of related voltage sensitive channels has been the subject of intense investigation both because of its physiological implications and because understanding the structural and biophysical mechanisms determining the different behaviors will reveal basic principles of voltage gating. This paper reports a combination of functional studies and molecular computations that proposes and supports a novel structural mechanism of voltage sensing and gating.
