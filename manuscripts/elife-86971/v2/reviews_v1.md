## Review text

DOI: [10.7554/eLife.86971.sa0](https://doi.org/10.7554/eLife.86971.sa0)

In this manuscript, the authors address an important and urgent question: what molecular mechanisms drive the invasive behavior of pancreatic adenocarcinoma? Because these tumors have such a strong propensity for invasion and metastasis, identifying actionable targets is of high importance. Using a combination of in silico and in vitro modeling, they identify a role for purinergic G-protein coupled receptor P2Y2 as a critical node in mediating PDAC invasion, and they find that blocking the crosstalk between P2Y2 and αV integrins via a peptide inhibitor blocks PDAC invasion, which may have clinical utility. Thus their study provides insights into both the basic biology of PDAC but also identifies a new target.
